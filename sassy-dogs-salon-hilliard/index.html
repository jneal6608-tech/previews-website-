Services

Everything a good groom needs

From a quick freshen-up to a full haircut, these are the kinds of grooming services dog owners usually come in for.

Bath & brush

A thorough wash, dry and brush-out to leave your dog clean, soft and smelling great.

Haircuts & styling

Full-body cuts, breed-style trims or a simple short and practical clip. Tell us the look you want.

Nails & paws

Nail trims and paw tidying for comfortable walking and fewer scratches on your floors.

Tidy-ups & touch-ups

Face, feet and sanitary trims to keep your dog neat between full grooms.

De-shedding & coat care

Brushing out loose undercoat and working through tangles to help with shedding.

Puppy grooms

A gentle introduction to the salon so your puppy can get used to baths, brushes and clippers.

Services and pricing can vary with size, breed and coat condition. Give us a call to confirm exactly what's available for your dog.

Free quote

Get a free grooming quote by phone

Every dog is different, so the quickest way to get an accurate quote is a short call. Have a few details ready and we'll take it from there.

Call for a free quote

Helpful to mention when you call

  • Your dog's breed (or best guess) and approximate size
  • Coat condition: any mats, tangles or heavy shedding
  • The look you want: bath only, tidy-up or full haircut
  • When your dog was last groomed
  • Anything we should know: age, nerves or sensitivities
