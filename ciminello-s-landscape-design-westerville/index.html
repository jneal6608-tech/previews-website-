What we do

Landscaping, from first sketch to finished yard

Every property is different. These are the kinds of projects a landscape design company typically takes on. Call to talk through what you have in mind.

Landscape design

Planning a layout that fits your home, your yard and the way you want to use it, so every piece works together.

Planting & garden beds

Trees, shrubs, perennials and seasonal color, arranged into beds and borders that give your property structure.

Patios & walkways

Hardscape features like patios, paths and steps that connect your outdoor spaces and make them easy to enjoy.

Walls & edging

Retaining walls, raised beds and clean edging to shape slopes, define spaces and keep everything tidy.

Lawns & groundcover

Establishing healthy turf or low-maintenance groundcover as part of a balanced overall design.

Outdoor living spaces

Bringing it all together into a backyard you'll actually spend time in, for gathering, relaxing and entertaining.
