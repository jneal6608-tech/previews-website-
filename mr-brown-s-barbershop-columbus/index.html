What we do

Barbershop services

The everyday work of a good barbershop, done with care. Call the shop to ask about a specific style or service.

Haircuts

Classic and modern men's cuts, from a tidy everyday trim to a full change of style.

Fades & Tapers

Low, mid or high, blended to suit your head shape and the look you're after.

Beard Trims

Shaping, trimming and tidying to keep your beard looking deliberate, not overgrown.

Shaves

A clean, close finish for the face and neck, the traditional barbershop way.

Line-Ups & Edge-Ups

Crisp hairlines and edges to keep a cut looking fresh between full appointments.

Kids' Cuts

Ask the shop about cuts for younger customers, from first haircuts to back-to-school.
